# Campus Layout, Building Directory & Step-by-Step Directions - GEC Hassan

## 1. Campus Geography & Main Entrances
- **Main Entrance Gate (Gate 1)**: Located right along the B.M. Road (Bangalore-Mangalore Highway), Hassan.
  - Security check-post and visitor registration desk are located at Gate 1.
  - The main paved driveway leads directly to the central circle / flag-post area facing the Administrative Block.
- **Rear / Service Gate (Gate 2)**: Located towards the northern side, primarily providing vehicular access to the Boys' Hostel, workshops, and sports ground.

---

## 2. Block-by-Block Building Directory & Exact Directions

### A. Administrative Block (Main Building - Ground & Upper Floors)
*Direction from Main Gate*: Walk straight along the main central avenue for approx. 80 meters. The prominent 3-storey building with the college portico is the Administrative Block.

- **Ground Floor (Right Wing)**:
  - **Principal's Office & Chamber**: Room 101, immediate right past the main foyer.
  - **Administrative Office & Student Section (Fee collection, Bonafide certificates, Admission desk)**: Room 103, right corridor.
  - **Examination Section & VTU Evaluation Centre**: Room 105, end of right corridor.
  - **Cash Counter & Accounts Section**: Room 106, right wing.
- **Ground Floor (Left Wing)**:
  - **Student Welfare Officer (SWO) Office**: Room 108.
  - **Faculty Board Room & Conference Hall**: Room 110.
  - **Visitor's Waiting Lounge & Helpdesk**: Ground floor entrance lobby.
- **First Floor (Administrative Block)**:
  - **Training & Placement Cell (TPC) & Interview Rooms**: Room 201, first floor right wing.
  - **Central Server Room & Campus Network Centre**: Room 204.
- **Second Floor (Administrative Block)**:
  - **Main College Auditorium / Multi-Purpose Seminar Hall**: Room 301 (Capacity: 450+ seats), equipped with AV projection and acoustic sound system.

### F. Hostels, Canteen & Student Amenities
- **College Canteen / Cafeteria**: Located centrally near the workshop walkway, providing hot vegetarian meals, breakfast, and snacks.
- **Boys' Hostel**: Located in the north-western sector of campus (approx. 200m walk from Main Building), equipped with dining hall and sports court.
- **Girls' Hostel**: Located in a dedicated, secure enclosure adjacent to the staff quarters on the eastern boundary with 24/7 security booth.
- **Sports Complex & Playground**: Situated behind the library block, featuring full-size cricket/football ground, volleyball court, and indoor gymnasium.
- **Girls' Common Room & Waiting Lounge**: Located on the Ground Floor of Academic Block 1 (Room 102) with restroom facilities and first-aid kit.
- **Boys' Common Room**: Located on Ground Floor of Academic Block 2 (Room C-102).

## 3. How to Reach GEC Hassan from City Transit Hubs
- **From Hassan Central KSRTC Bus Stand (Distance: ~4 km)**:
  - Take any town bus (Red Bus) heading towards B.M. Road / Shantigrama route and alight at "Government Engineering College Stop".
  - Auto-rickshaws are available 24/7 directly from the bus stand prepaid auto counter (approx. 10-12 minutes journey).
  - Dedicated GEC college buses depart the KSRTC Bus Stand at 8:30 AM and 8:45 AM every morning.
- **From Hassan Junction Railway Station (Distance: ~5 km)**:
  - Auto-rickshaw directly from the station exit to GEC Hassan campus gate.
  - Local city bus from Railway Station Circle to B.M. Road college stop.
