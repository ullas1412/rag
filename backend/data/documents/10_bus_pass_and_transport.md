# Transportation, Student Bus Pass & Commuting Guide - GEC Hassan

## 1. KSRTC Student Concessional Bus Pass (Seva Sindhu)
Most students commuting from Hassan city, nearby taluks (Arsikere, Channarayapatna, Holenarasipura, Sakleshpur, Alur, Belur) utilize the Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC) student concessional bus pass.

### Step-by-Step Application Procedure:
1. **Online Application**: Apply through the Karnataka Seva Sindhu portal ([https://sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in](https://sevasindhuservices.karnataka.gov.in)).
2. **College Verification**: Enter your Student Enrollment / USN number, select "Government Engineering College Hassan" as the institution, and upload your current year college fee receipt and passport photograph.
3. **Institutional Approval**: The college administrative office (Student Section) verifies the online application within 2-3 working days.
4. **Pass Collection**: Once approved by the college and KSRTC depot, pay the nominal concessional fee online or at the Hassan Central KSRTC Bus Stand pass counter to receive the smart card bus pass.

## 2. College Bus Services
- GEC Hassan operates dedicated college buses running on key routes across Hassan city:
  - **Route 1**: Hassan Central KSRTC Bus Stand -> Shankar Mutt -> N.R. Circle -> B.M. Road -> GEC Hassan Campus.
  - **Route 2**: Hassan Railway Station -> Salagame Road -> Dairy Circle -> Ring Road -> GEC Campus.
  - **Route 3**: Channapatna Bypass -> Housing Board -> New Bus Stand -> College.
- **Timings**: Buses arrive at campus by 8:45 AM before 9:00 AM morning classes, and depart campus at 5:10 PM after afternoon lab sessions.

## 3. Local Auto & Town Bus Connectivity
- City town buses (Red buses) regularly ply on the B.M. Road route connecting Hassan city centre to the college gate.
- Auto-rickshaws are readily available at fixed nominal rates from Hassan city bus stand and railway station.
