# VTU Marks Calculation, SGPA/CGPA Formulas & Passing Rules

## 1. Passing Marks Calculation in Theory Courses (50 CIE + 50 SEE = 100 Total)
Under VTU regulations followed at GEC Hassan:
- **Maximum Marks**: Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) = 50 Marks, Semester End Exam (SEE) = 50 Marks (written exam out of 100 scaled down to 50).
- **Minimum Marks Required to Pass**:
  1. **In SEE (Semester End Exam)**: Minimum **35% of 50 marks = 18 marks** (or 35 out of 100 in the written exam).
  2. **In CIE (Internal Assessment)**: Minimum **40% of 50 marks = 20 marks**.
  3. **Total Aggregate (CIE + SEE)**: Minimum **40% of 100 marks = 40 marks**.
- *Example Scenarios*:
  - If a student scores 22 in CIE, they need at least 18 in SEE (Total = 40) -> **PASS**.
  - If a student scores 18 in CIE: They are detained from taking SEE because CIE is below the 20 mark (40%) threshold.
  - If a student scores 45 in CIE and 16 in SEE: Even though total is 61 (> 40), they fail because SEE is below the 18 mark (35%) threshold.

## 2. SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) Formula
$$SGPA = \frac{\sum (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum C_i}$$
Where:
- $C_i$ = Credits assigned to the $i$-th course (e.g., 3 credits for Theory, 1 credit for Lab).
- $G_i$ = Grade points secured in the $i$-th course based on the 10-point scale:
  - 90–100% -> Grade Point 10 (Grade O)
  - 80–89%  -> Grade Point 9  (Grade A+)
  - 70–79%  -> Grade Point 8  (Grade A)
  - 60–69%  -> Grade Point 7  (Grade B+)
  - 55–59%  -> Grade Point 6  (Grade B)
  - 50–54%  -> Grade Point 5  (Grade C)
  - 40–49%  -> Grade Point 4  (Grade P)
  - Below 40% -> Grade Point 0 (Grade F - Fail)

## 3. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) Formula
$$CGPA = \frac{\sum (C_i \times G_i) \text{ across all completed semesters}}{\sum C_i \text{ across all completed semesters}}$$

## 4. CGPA to Percentage Conversion Formula (VTU Standard)
$$\text{Percentage } (\%) = (\text{CGPA} - 0.75) \times 10$$
*Example*:
- A CGPA of 8.0 = $(8.0 - 0.75) \times 10 = 7.25 \times 10 = 72.5\%$.
- A CGPA of 6.75 = $(6.75 - 0.75) \times 10 = 6.00 \times 10 = 60.0\%$.

## 5. Degree Classification
- **First Class with Distinction (FCD)**: CGPA $\ge$ 7.75 (and cleared all subjects in first attempt).
- **First Class (FC)**: CGPA $\ge$ 6.75.
- **Second Class (SC)**: CGPA $\ge$ 5.75 and < 6.75.
- **Pass Class**: CGPA $\ge$ 4.00 and < 5.75.
