# Academic Regulations and VTU Rules at GEC Hassan

## 1. Credit-Based Semester System (CBSS) & NEP Framework
GEC Hassan follows the curriculum, grading guidelines, and academic regulations prescribed by Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi.

## 2. Attendance Requirement
- **Mandatory Minimum Attendance**: Every student must maintain a minimum of **85% attendance** in each individual subject/course (both theory and practical classes).
- **Condonation / Medical Exemption**: A condonation of attendance up to **10%** (i.e., minimum 75% attendance) may be granted by the Principal/University on genuine medical grounds (supported by medical certificates) or approved participation in university/state/national sports, NCC, NSS, or cultural events.
- **Detention**: Students with less than 75% attendance under any circumstances are declared as "NSAR" (Not Satisfied Attendance Requirement) and are detained from appearing in the Semester End Examination (SEE) for that course.

## 3. Evaluation Scheme: CIE and SEE
Each course is assessed through two main components:
1. **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)**: 50% weightage
   - Conducted through 3 Internal Assessment (IA) tests, quizzes, assignments, case studies, or mini-projects.
   - Minimum eligibility in CIE: Students must secure a minimum of 40% in CIE to be eligible to appear for the Semester End Examination (SEE).
2. **Semester End Examination (SEE)**: 50% weightage
   - Conducted centrally by Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) at the end of each semester.
   - Question papers are set externally and answer scripts undergo coded digital evaluation.

## 4. Passing Criteria & Grading System
- **Passing Minimum**:
  - A student must secure at least **35% marks in SEE** (Semester End Exam) and an aggregate of at least **40% marks combining CIE and SEE** together to pass the course.
- **Letter Grades and Grade Points**:
  - `O` (Outstanding): 90–100% (Grade Point: 10)
  - `A+` (Excellent): 80–89% (Grade Point: 9)
  - `A` (Very Good): 70–79% (Grade Point: 8)
  - `B+` (Good): 60–69% (Grade Point: 7)
  - `B` (Above Average): 55–59% (Grade Point: 6)
  - `C` (Average): 50–54% (Grade Point: 5)
  - `P` (Pass): 40–49% (Grade Point: 4)
  - `F` (Fail): Below 40% (Grade Point: 0)
- **SGPA & CGPA Calculation**:
  - SGPA = Sum of (Credit x Grade Point) / Total Semester Credits.
  - CGPA = Cumulative grade point average across all completed semesters.

## 5. Promotion Policy and Vertical Progression
- Under VTU guidelines, a student must not carry more than 4 backlogs (F grades) from the first year to enter the 3rd year (5th semester), and must have cleared all 1st year subjects to enter the final year (7th semester).
