# Admissions, Fee Structure, and Scholarships at GEC Hassan

## 1. Admission Modes
- **First Year B.E.**: Admissions are conducted through Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA) based on the Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) ranks.
- **Lateral Entry (2nd Year B.E.)**: Diploma holders are admitted directly to the 3rd semester via the Diploma CET (DCET) conducted by KEA.
- **Supernumerary Quotas**: SNQ (Supernumerary Quota for economically weaker meritorious students), Hyderabad-Karnataka (HK / Article 371J) reservation, rural quota, Kannada medium quota, and sports/defense quotas are strictly implemented per Govt. of Karnataka norms.

## 2. Fee Structure (Government Subsidized)
As a government institution, GEC Hassan offers highly affordable fees set by the Government of Karnataka:
- **General / OBC Category KCET Tuition & College Fee**: Approx. ₹38,000 to ₹43,000 per year (subject to annual DTE/KEA notification).
- **SC / ST / Category-1 Candidates**: Substantially subsidized or 100% fee exemption as per Social Welfare Department post-matric scholarship guidelines.
- **SNQ Quota Students**: Pay a nominal fee (around ₹4,000 to ₹6,000 per annum) with tuition fee fully waived.
- **Hostel Fees**: Approx. ₹18,000 to ₹22,000 per year for room rent and maintenance + monthly mess bill on a dividing system (typically ₹2,500 - ₹3,200/month).

## 3. Scholarships Available
Students at GEC Hassan are eligible for multiple central and state government scholarships:

### A. State Scholarship Portal (SSP - Karnataka)
- **Eligibility**: Karnataka domicile students belonging to SC, ST, Minorities, and Backward Classes (OBC/OBC Cat-1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B).
- **Benefits**: Reimburses tuition fees and provides maintenance allowance directly into the student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
- **Website & Portal**: [https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in](https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in)
- **Required Documents**: Aadhaar Card, Caste & Income Certificate, SSLC/10th Marks card, PUC/12th Marks card, College Fee Receipt, Student Bank Passbook (NPCI mapped).

### B. National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
- **Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students**: Merit-based scholarship for students scoring above the 80th percentile in Class 12.
- **Post-Matric Scholarship for Minorities** (Muslim, Christian, Jain, Buddhist, Sikh, Parsi).
- **Pragati Scholarship**: AICTE scholarship exclusively for meritorious girl students admitted to technical degree courses (₹50,000 per year).
- **Saksham Scholarship**: AICTE scholarship for specially-abled students (disability > 40%).

### C. Vidyasiri (Epass Karnataka) & Free Hostel Stipend
- Food and Accommodation Scheme (FAAS) for eligible backward classes students who do not secure government hostel admission.
