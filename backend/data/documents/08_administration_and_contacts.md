# Administration, Committees & Contact Directory - GEC Hassan

## 1. Key Administrative Officers
- **Principal**: Head of the Institution, Academic & Administrative Authority.
- **Vice-Principal / Academic Dean**: Oversees academic scheduling, VTU compliance, curriculum execution.
- **Head of Departments (HODs)**:
  - HOD Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
  - HOD Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
  - HOD Mechanical Engineering (ME)
  - HOD Civil Engineering (CV)
  - HOD Basic Sciences & Humanities (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)
- **Training & Placement Officer (TPO)**: Placement & internship coordination.
- **Student Welfare Officer (SWO)**: Student grievance handling, scholarship facilitation, extracurricular support.
- **Hostel Wardens**: Chief Warden (Boys Hostel) and Chief Warden (Girls Hostel).

## 2. Statutory Committees & Student Grievances
- **Anti-Ragging Committee & Squad**: GEC Hassan maintains a strict Zero Tolerance Policy against ragging per UGC and Supreme Court regulations.
  - National Anti-Ragging Helpline: 1800-180-5522 (Toll Free)
- **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) / Women Empowerment Cell**: Prevents sexual harassment and ensures safe, gender-inclusive campus.
- **SC/ST & OBC Grievance Cell**: Redresses complaints and ensures proper welfare implementation.
- **Student Grievance Redressal Cell (SGRC)**: Provides an open platform to address academic and infrastructural concerns.
