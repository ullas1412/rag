# Academic Departments & Undergraduate Programs at GEC Hassan

## 1. Undergraduate B.E. (Bachelor of Engineering) Programs Offered
All programs are 4-year (8 semesters) full-time degree courses affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) and approved by AICTE.

### 1. Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
- **Program**: B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering
- **Annual Intake**: 60 seats (plus lateral entry quota)
- **Specialized Laboratories**: Data Structures & Algorithms Lab, Advanced Programming Lab (Python/Java/C++), Database Management Systems (DBMS) Lab, Machine Learning & AI Lab, Web Development & Cloud Computing Lab, Computer Networks & IoT Lab.
- **Key Areas of Focus**: Full-stack Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Big Data, Cloud Computing, Mobile Application Development.
- **Student Association**: "GLITCH" (CSE Student Forum) organizing technical symposiums, hackathons, coding contests, and guest lectures.

### 2. Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
- **Program**: B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering
- **Annual Intake**: 60 seats
- **Specialized Laboratories**: Analog & Digital Electronics Lab, Microcontroller & Embedded Systems Lab (ARM, 8051, Arduino), Digital Signal Processing (DSP) Lab, VLSI Design Lab (Cadence/Xilinx), Communication Systems & Microwave Lab.
- **Key Areas of Focus**: Embedded Systems, IoT, VLSI Design, Robotics, 5G Wireless Communications, Signal Processing.

### 3. Department of Mechanical Engineering (ME)
- **Program**: B.E. in Mechanical Engineering
- **Annual Intake**: 60 seats
- **Specialized Laboratories**: Computer Aided Design & Drafting (CADD/CAM) Lab, Material Testing & Metallography Lab, Heat Transfer & Thermodynamics Lab, Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulic Machinery Lab, Machine Shop & Foundry/Forging Workshop.
- **Key Areas of Focus**: Automation, Thermal Engineering, Automotive Technology, Renewable Energy, CNC Machining, Mechatronics.

### 4. Department of Civil Engineering (CV)
- **Program**: B.E. in Civil Engineering
- **Annual Intake**: 60 seats
- **Specialized Laboratories**: Surveying Lab (Total Station, Theodolite), Concrete and Highway Materials Testing Lab, Geotechnical / Soil Mechanics Lab, Environmental Engineering Lab, Computer Aided Building Planning Lab.
- **Key Areas of Focus**: Structural Engineering, Green Building Technologies, Water Resources, Transportation Engineering, GIS & Remote Sensing.

### 5. Basic Sciences and Humanities Department
- **Sections**: Engineering Mathematics, Engineering Physics, Engineering Chemistry, and Humanities & Social Sciences (Constitution of India, Professional Ethics, Technical English, Communicative Kannada).
- **Laboratories**: Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, Language and Communication Skills Laboratory.
