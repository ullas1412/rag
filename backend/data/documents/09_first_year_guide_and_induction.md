# First Year B.E. Student Induction & Academic Guide - GEC Hassan

## 1. Welcome & Mandatory Induction Program
- **Induction Program**: As mandated by AICTE and VTU, every newly admitted 1st year B.E. student undergoes a 2-to-3 week mandatory Student Induction Program (SIP).
- **Activities**: Campus orientation, universal human values (UHV), creative arts, sports, physical fitness, familiarization with departments and laboratories, mentorship allotment, and visits to Hassan district heritage and industrial sites (e.g., Belur, Halebidu, ISRO Master Control Facility).

## 2. First Year Course Cycles: Physics Cycle vs Chemistry Cycle
Under the VTU NEP / CBSS curriculum, the first year engineering students are divided into two parallel groups:
1. **Physics Group / Cycle**:
   - Engineering Physics & Lab
   - Mathematics-I & Mathematics-II (Calculus and Linear Algebra)
   - Basic Electrical Engineering / Electronics
   - Elements of Mechanical Engineering
   - Computer Aided Engineering Drawing (CAED)
   - Communicative English / Professional Writing
2. **Chemistry Group / Cycle**:
   - Engineering Chemistry & Lab
   - Problem Solving through C / Python Programming & Lab
   - Basic Electronics Engineering
   - Elements of Civil Engineering and Mechanics
   - Technical English / Scientific Writing
   - Constitution of India and Professional Ethics (CIP)
- *Note*: Groups swap cycles in the 2nd semester, ensuring all students complete the foundation courses before entering the 3rd semester (branch-specific).

## 3. Approved Calculator Policy
- Only non-programmable scientific calculators are permitted inside examination halls:
  - Recommended models: Casio fx-991ES Plus, Casio fx-82MS, or equivalent.
  - Programmable calculators (e.g., Casio fx-9860, fx-5800P) and mobile phone calculator apps are strictly prohibited.

## 4. Branch Change Policy (After 1st Year)
- Eligible students who have passed all subjects in the 1st and 2nd semesters with a high CGPA (typically > 8.0 CGPA) can apply for a change of branch at the end of the 1st year, subject to vacancy in the target department (e.g., CSE or ECE) and VTU notification.
- Branch change is merit-based through the official VTU portal and approved by DTE Karnataka.
