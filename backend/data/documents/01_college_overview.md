# Government Engineering College Hassan (GEC Hassan) - Institutional Overview

## 1. About the College
Government Engineering College, Hassan (often abbreviated as GEC Hassan or GECH) is a premier government technical education institution established in 2007 by the Government of Karnataka under the Department of Technical Education (DTE).
The college is dedicated to providing quality engineering education to students, especially from rural and economically challenged backgrounds, empowering them with technical knowledge, professional ethics, and lifelong learning capabilities.

## 2. Affiliation and Approvals
- **Affiliation**: Affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, Karnataka.
- **Accreditation / Approval**: Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.
- **Governing Body**: Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Department of Higher Education, Government of Karnataka.
- **VTU College Code**: 4HN
- **KCET Code**: E146

## 3. Location and Campus
- **Campus Address**: B.M. Road (Bangalore-Mangalore Highway), Hassan, Karnataka - 573201.
- **Campus Setting**: Situated in a serene, eco-friendly campus with modern academic blocks, well-equipped laboratories, administrative building, sports grounds, and hostel facilities.
- **Connectivity**: Located close to Hassan Central Bus Stand (approx. 4 km) and Hassan Junction Railway Station (approx. 5 km), with frequent local city buses and private auto-rickshaws available.

## 4. Vision and Mission
- **Vision**: To be an institute of repute producing globally competent, ethically sound, and socially responsible engineering professionals to contribute to the technological and societal advancement of the nation.
- **Mission**:
  1. Impart quality technical education through state-of-the-art curriculum, modern pedagogical methods, and continuous faculty development.
  2. Provide world-class laboratory facilities and encourage practical research, innovation, and entrepreneurship.
  3. Cultivate industry-institute partnerships to enhance industry readiness, internships, and high-value employment.
  4. Inculcate professional ethics, leadership qualities, and environmental consciousness among students.
