# Training, Placements & Internships at GEC Hassan

## 1. Training & Placement Cell (TPC)
The Training and Placement Cell at GEC Hassan acts as a vital bridge between graduating students and prospective industry recruiters. The cell is headed by a Training & Placement Officer (TPO) assisted by departmental faculty coordinators and student placement ambassadors.

## 2. Pre-Placement Training & Skill Development
- **Aptitude & Reasoning Training**: Quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and verbal ability workshops conducted from the 4th semester onward.
- **Technical Bootcamp**: Hands-on coding in Python, Java, C++, Data Structures, SQL, Web Stack, and Cloud Fundamentals.
- **Soft Skills & Professional Grooming**: Communication enhancement, group discussions (GD), mock technical and HR interviews, resume building, and LinkedIn profiling.

## 3. Placement Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum aggregate CGPA: Typically 6.0 to 6.5+ CGPA (60% to 65% across all semesters).
- Backlog Policy: Most tier-1 recruiters require no active/standing backlogs at the time of campus drives.
- Consistent academic record: Minimum 60% in 10th (SSLC) and 12th / PUC / Diploma.

## 4. Prominent Recruiters & Job Roles
- **Software / IT Services & Product Companies**: Infosys, Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), Wipro, Cognizant, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Mindtree / LTIMindtree, HCL Technologies, Quest Global.
- **Core Engineering Recruiters**: Bosch, Toyota Kirloskar Auto Parts, Schneider Electric, Larsen & Toubro (L&T), Jindal Steel (JSW), Continental Automotive.
- **Job Roles**: Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET), Software Engineer, Systems Engineer, Embedded Developer, Quality Assurance Engineer, Junior Design Engineer.
- **Salary Packages**: Average CTC ranges from ₹3.5 LPA to ₹5.5 LPA, with dream offers up to ₹8.0 - ₹10.0+ LPA.

## 5. Mandatory VTU Internship Policy
- As per VTU guidelines, every student must complete mandatory industrial internships:
  - Mini-project / Innovation / Social Connect Internship at 3rd/4th semester level.
  - Research / Industry Internship (minimum 4 to 6 weeks) during the 6th/7th semester.
- Students must submit an official internship certificate, logbook, and comprehensive project report, followed by a viva-voce examination.
