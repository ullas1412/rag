# Examination Section & Evaluation Procedures at GEC Hassan

## 1. Structure of Examinations
- Examinations are held twice every academic year: Odd Semester Exams (typically December–January) and Even Semester Exams (May–July).
- Both theory and laboratory/practical exams are administered according to the official timetable published by Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU).

## 2. Examination Registration & Hall Ticket (Admission Ticket)
- **Exam Form Submission**: Students must register online through the VTU Examination Portal within the dates notified by the college examination section.
- **Exam Fee**: Exam fees per paper and marks card charges must be deposited online or through designated bank challans before the deadline.
- **Hall Ticket Generation**: Hall tickets are issued to students who have:
  1. Completed exam form registration with requisite fees paid.
  2. Met the minimum attendance threshold (85% or condoned 75%).
  3. Secured passing marks in CIE (minimum 40%).
- Hall tickets must be collected from the respective department office or downloaded via the VTU portal with the Principal's endorsement.

## 3. Revaluation, Retotaling & Photocopy
- **Photocopy of Answer Script**: Students can apply for digital copies of their evaluated theory answer scripts within 7-10 days of result announcement.
- **Revaluation**: If a student is unsatisfied with their marks, they can apply for official revaluation through the college exam section portal.
- **Challenge Valuation**: In exceptional cases, students may opt for challenge revaluation per VTU regulations.

## 4. Backlog and Makeup Examinations
- Students with course backlogs can register for supplementary/arrear examinations in subsequent odd or even examination cycles.
- Fast-track / Summer semesters may be announced by VTU for eligible students to clear pending subjects.

## 5. Academic Malpractice & Code of Conduct
- Carrying mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, notes, or unauthorized chits inside the examination hall is strictly prohibited.
- Any malpractice is reported to the VTU Malpractice Committee, leading to penalties ranging from paper cancellation to debarment.
