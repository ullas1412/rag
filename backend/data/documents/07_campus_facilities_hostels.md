# Campus Infrastructure & Student Facilities at GEC Hassan

## 1. Central Library and Information Centre
- **Collection**: Extensive collection of over 25,000+ textbooks, reference volumes, competitive examination guides (GATE, GRE, CAT), and national/international journals.
- **Digital Library & VTU Consortium**: Access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, Springer, ASCE, ASME, and Taylor & Francis e-journals through the VTU e-Learning Consortium.
- **Book Bank Scheme**: Dedicated book bank facility catering to SC/ST and economically disadvantaged students, providing complete sets of textbooks for the entire semester.

## 2. Hostels & Accommodation
- **Boys' Hostel**: Located near the campus with furnished rooms (double and triple occupancy), study tables, Wi-Fi connectivity, RO drinking water, and 24/7 security.
- **Girls' Hostel**: Located with secured boundaries, dedicated female warden, CCTV surveillance, solar water heaters, medical first-aid, and biometric attendance.
- **Hostel Mess**: Serves hygienic South Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian food operating on a non-profit dividing system.

## 3. Sports & Gymnasium
- **Outdoor Facilities**: Dedicated grounds for Cricket, Football, Volleyball, Basketball, and Track & Field athletics.
- **Indoor Games**: Badminton court, Table Tennis, Carrom, and Chess.
- **Gymnasium**: Equipped with modern fitness machines and free weights.
- Annual Sports Meet is held every year, with students participating in VTU Inter-Collegiate Tournaments.

## 4. Student Clubs & Extracurricular Activities
- **NSS (National Service Scheme)**: Active wing conducting blood donation camps, rural health awareness, plantation drives, and social service camps.
- **Red Cross Society & Youth Red Cross (YRC)**: First aid training, disaster response workshops.
- **Cultural Forum**: Organizes the annual college cultural fest featuring dance, music, theater, fine arts, and literary competitions.

## 5. Transportation and Canteen
- **College Bus Facility**: Regular bus services connecting key locations in Hassan City, including Hassan Central Bus Stand, Railway Station, Salagame Road, and Channapatna.
- **Cafeteria / Canteen**: Modern canteen providing hygienic breakfast, meals, tea/coffee, and snacks at government-subsidized student rates.
- **Wi-Fi Campus**: High-speed broadband Internet connectivity available throughout academic blocks and departments.
