# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - GEC Hassan Student Guide

### Q1: What is the college timing and working days?
College operates from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Saturday. The college is closed on 2nd and 4th Saturdays, all Sundays, and notified Karnataka Government public holidays.

### Q2: What is the VTU college code and KCET entrance code for GEC Hassan?
- VTU University Code: **4HN**
- KCET Counseling Code: **E146**

### Q3: What should I do if my attendance drops below 85%?
If your attendance is between 75% and 84%, submit a medical certificate or duty leave certificate signed by the competent doctor/faculty coordinator to your department HOD and Principal for condonation approval before the CIE freeze date. If attendance falls below 75%, you will be detained under the NSAR (Not Satisfied Attendance Requirement) category.

### Q4: How do I get my Bonafide / Study Certificate for scholarships or passport?
Visit the Academic & Student Section in the Administrative Block with your student ID card. Submit a written requisition mentioning your USN, current branch, semester, and the purpose (SSP Scholarship, Bus Pass, Education Loan, Passport). Certificates are usually issued within 24 to 48 hours.

### Q5: How do I apply for the SSP Post-Matric Scholarship?
1. Visit [https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in](https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in).
2. Create an account using your Aadhaar number (must be linked to mobile and bank account via NPCI).
3. Enter your College Registration Number / USN, caste certificate number, and income certificate number.
4. Submit the online application and submit the printed acknowledgment with fee receipt to the college scholarship officer.

### Q6: Can I apply for revaluation if I am not happy with my VTU semester exam marks?
Yes. Within 7 to 10 days of VTU result declaration, apply for photocopy of the answer script or direct revaluation via the VTU examination portal by paying the prescribed university revaluation fee.

### Q7: What are the hostel fee payment and mess rules?
Hostel room rent and maintenance fees are paid annually at the beginning of each academic year (approx. ₹18,000 to ₹22,000). Mess bills are calculated every month on a dividing system (approx. ₹2,500 to ₹3,200 per month) and must be cleared by the 10th of the subsequent month.

### Q8: What are the placement eligibility criteria for campus drives?
Most companies require a minimum of 60% or 6.5 CGPA throughout 10th, 12th/PUC, and B.E., with no active backlogs at the time of the recruitment process.

### Q9: Whom should I contact in case of ragging or harassment?
GEC Hassan has a zero-tolerance policy against ragging. Contact:
- Anti-Ragging Committee Head / Principal Office
- National Anti-Ragging Helpline (Toll-Free): **1800-180-5522**
- Women Empowerment / Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)

### Q10: How can I change my engineering branch after the 1st year?
Branch change applications open after 2nd-semester results are declared by VTU. You must have cleared all 1st and 2nd-semester subjects in the first attempt. Allocation is strictly merit-based against vacant seats in the desired department.
